# Privacy Policy for Joeshift

**Last Updated:** November 2026

Joeshift ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy and is dedicated to protecting user data. This Privacy Policy details how data is handled across our mobile applications.

---

### 1. Information Collection and Storage
**Joeshift does NOT collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal information.**

- **On-Device Data**: All gameplay progress, high scores, earned cyber credits, unlocked craft skins, and audio/control preferences are saved strictly on your local device storage using standard application sandboxes (`AsyncStorage`).
- **No Remote Servers**: Joeshift does not operate remote servers that capture user telemetry, contact details, device fingerprints, or GPS coordinates.

---

### 2. Device Permissions
The application may utilize basic device audio playback features to output synthesized real-time sound effects and cyber music. We do not record or transmit ambient room audio or personal speech.

---

### 3. Advertising & Analytics
- **No Third-Party Advertising**: Joeshift contains zero third-party advertisements or ad tracking SDKs.
- **No Behavioral Trackers**: Joeshift does not utilize Google Advertising ID (AAID) or Apple Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA).

---

### 4. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)
Joeshift complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and international child data protection standards. We do not knowingly solicit, collect, or store personal information from children under the age of 13.

---

### 5. Data Deletion
Since all game data is kept exclusively on your local device, you have complete control over your data at all times. You can permanently delete all stored statistics, scores, and preferences at any time simply by clearing the application storage via your device settings or by uninstalling the application.
