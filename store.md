## 7. Android Permissions & Review Considerations

In the current `app.json` configuration, the following permissions are listed under `android.permissions`:
```json
"permissions": [
  "android.permission.RECORD_AUDIO",
  "android.permission.MODIFY_AUDIO_SETTINGS",
  "android.permission.FOREGROUND_SERVICE",
  "android.permission.FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK"
]
```

> [!WARNING]
> ### Google Play Review Warning regarding `RECORD_AUDIO` and `FOREGROUND_SERVICE`
> Google Play Console has strict policies regarding **Microphone (`RECORD_AUDIO`)** and **Foreground Services**.
> - **Microphone**: If your game does not have voice recording or audio visualization based on microphone input, Google Play review will require a detailed justification or will reject the submission.
> - **Recommendation**: If microphone input is not used by game mechanics, remove `"android.permission.RECORD_AUDIO"` and `"android.permission.FOREGROUND_SERVICE"` from `app.json` before final production submission to ensure instant, frictionless Google Play approval.

## 9. Pre-Submission Checklist & Step-by-Step Upload Guide

### 9.1 Google Play Store (Production Track)
1. **Log in to [Google Play Console](https://play.google.com/console)**.
2. Select your developer account (`Yousif Hamid`).
3. Click **Create app**:
   - App Name: `Joeshift: Cyber Neon Runner`
   - Default language: `English (United States)`
   - App or game: `Game`
   - Free or paid: `Free`
4. Complete the **Set up your app** tasks:
   - **Privacy Policy**: Enter link to `PRIVACY_POLICY.md`.
   - **App Access**: All functionality is available without special access.
   - **Ads**: Declare "No, my app does not contain ads".
   - **Content Rating**: Complete questionnaire (select Game > Action; rating will be 3+ / E).
   - **Target Audience**: Select ages 13 and above (or 9-12 with COPPA compliance).
   - **Data Safety**: Declare that no user data is collected or shared.
5. In **Main Store Listing**:
   - Paste the Title, Short Description, and Full Description from Section 2.
   - Upload the 512x512 Icon (`google-play-icon.png`).
   - Upload the 1024x500 Feature Graphic.
   - Upload phone screenshots (1080x1920).
6. In **Production > Releases**:
   - Create a new release.
   - Download the generated `.aab` file from your EAS Build dashboard:
     🔗 [EAS AAB Build #c7d63002](https://expo.dev/accounts/yousif.hamid/projects/joeshift/builds/c7d63002-15fa-41ef-9726-8f8e8e4a33d3)
   - Upload the `.aab` file.
   - Enter Release Notes: `Initial official release of Joeshift (v1.0.0).`
   - Click **Review and Roll out to Production**!

1. **Log in to [App Store Connect](https://appstoreconnect.apple.com)**.
2. Go to **Apps** and click the `+` button -> **New App**.
   - Platforms: `iOS`
